# Data licence

The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The benchmark
items are **not**: every item is derived from Fedlex, the Swiss federal law
portal (https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/), and carries this notice in its
`licence` field:

> Fedlex data may be reused free of charge with source attribution

Reuse of the items, in whole or in part, must keep the attribution to
Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch). Each item's `gold.eli` / `distractor.eli` is the
ELI of the exact consolidated edition its text was taken from. See
`CARD.md`, "Source and licence".
